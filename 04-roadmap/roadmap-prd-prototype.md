# Feature Roadmap, Module 4 · StreamLine Spotlight

**Team:** 2 engineers + 1 designer

## Strategic anchors
- **Persona:** A passionate film enthusiast who values cinematic quality, originality and discovering hidden gems beyond mainstream recommendations.
- **Primary metric:** Curated Content Discovery-to-Play Conversion Rate
- **Moment of misery:** Marcus opens StreamLine hoping to discover something new but finds repetitive franchise recommendations based on a single viewing choice, leaving him feeling misunderstood and wishing for the human-curated discoveries he once enjoyed.
- **Guardrail:** Average Weekly Viewing Sessions per Power User
