- **Is the moment of misery a systemic problem or just an edge case?:** StreamLine’s discovery challenge is likely a systemic problem rather than an isolated edge case. Its long-standing focus on content volume over quality of discovery may have created recurring frustration for users who struggle to find relevant, worthwhile films among thousands of titles. Plateauing engagement and rising churn suggest this could be a broader issue rooted in the platform’s strategy, rather than an occasional usability problem.

However, further research is needed to establish the scale and impact. The problem may be concentrated among high-value film enthusiasts rather than affecting all subscribers equally. Even if it affects a smaller segment, the business impact could be significant if these users contribute disproportionately to revenue and retention. Validating the frequency of this frustration, its relationship to churn and the needs of affected users will help determine whether Spotlight is the right solution.
- **Does the value proposition actually remove the obstacle you identified, or is it a generic feature?:** StreamLine Spotlight's value proposition directly addresses the identified obstacle, but its effectiveness still needs validation. The core problem is not a lack of content, but the difficulty of discovering relevant, high-quality films within an overwhelming library. By introducing expert human curation, carefully selected collections and trusted recommendations, Spotlight aims to reduce choice overload, minimise time spent searching and restore confidence in StreamLine as a destination for film discovery.

However, curation alone risks becoming a generic feature if it simply introduces another category or collection of recommended films. To deliver meaningful value, Spotlight must offer a genuinely differentiated discovery experience that feels personal, credible and engaging. Its success should be measured by whether users find worthwhile content more easily, watch more consistently and are less likely to cancel their subscriptions. The key is to validate that Spotlight removes the underlying frustration rather than simply adding another feature to an already complex platform.
