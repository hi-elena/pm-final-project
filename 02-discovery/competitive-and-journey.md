## Responses
- **Role, who are you solving for? (the specific user segment or profile):** A passionate film enthusiast who values cinematic quality, originality and discovering hidden gems beyond mainstream recommendations.
- **Goal, what is this user ultimately trying to achieve?:** To effortlessly discover exceptional, personally relevant films through trusted, thoughtful curation.
- **Friction, the main barrier (moment of misery) stopping them from succeeding:** Marcus opens StreamLine hoping to discover something new but finds repetitive franchise recommendations based on a single viewing choice, leaving him feeling misunderstood and wishing for the human-curated discoveries he once enjoyed.
- **External tools, the outside platforms or tools the user is forced to use:** Important distinction: Marcus's interview confirms his frustration with recommendations and preference for human curation, but does not explicitly describe which external tools he uses. The following is a plausible workaround journey, informed by the wider user research.
- **The process, the 3 to 5 manual steps the user takes to get the job done:** Step-by-step workaround journey
1. Opens StreamLine to find something interesting
   
   Marcus browses recommended titles but encounters repetitive action sequels and mainstream films that don't reflect his broader interests.
   
   Evidence: UXR-02, BUG-1091
2. Searches externally for inspiration
   
   He might turn to Google, film review sites, Letterboxd or curated film publications to discover critically acclaimed films and hidden gems.
3. Manually researches potential films
   
   He reads reviews, explores curated lists and compares recommendations to identify films that match his personal tastes.
4. Returns to StreamLine to check availability
   
   Once he finds something interesting, he searches StreamLine for the title. If it isn't available, or is difficult to find, he may need to look elsewhere.
   
   Related evidence: UXR-07, BUG-1080
5. Switches to another platform
   
   If a competitor provides a more convenient curated experience, Marcus may choose to watch there instead of continuing his search on StreamLine.
   
   Related evidence: UXR-04 — Tom cancelled in favour of a curated competitor
- **Core frustration, the exact moment the process feels most “broken”:** Marcus realises StreamLine keeps recommending repetitive, irrelevant films, forcing him to look elsewhere for meaningful recommendations rather than discovering something worth watching on the platform.
- **The evidence, a specific quote or behavior from the research that proves this:** UXR-02 (Marcus): “I'm not a genre, I'm a person. I miss when a human curator would surface a hidden gem.” Supported by BUG-1091, which confirms repetitive, low-diversity recommendations.
