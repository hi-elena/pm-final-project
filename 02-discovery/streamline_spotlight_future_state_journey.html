Strategy: Restore brand authority through human-led curation rather than mass-market scale.

From algorithmic frustration to trusted, effortless film discovery.

The four stages

1. Discover Spotlight

  • User Action: Opens Spotlight's editorial homepage → Sees hand-picked films instead of repetitive recommendations.
  • Internal State: Feels curious and understood → Trust in StreamLine begins to return.
  • Pain Point Addressed: Generic recommendations → Relevant, human-curated discovery.

2. Explore Curated Collections

  • User Action: Browses themed collections and curator notes → Discovers hidden gems matching his interests.
  • Internal State: Feels inspired → Confidence grows in the quality of recommendations.
  • Pain Point Addressed: Overwhelming choice → Smaller, meaningful collections replace endless scrolling.

3. Choose with Confidence

  • User Action: Reads a curator's recommendation → Selects a film without researching external reviews.
  • Internal State: Feels reassured → Trust replaces uncertainty and decision fatigue.
  • Pain Point Addressed: External research across platforms → Trusted recommendations available directly within StreamLine.

4. Watch and Rediscover

  • User Action: Watches his chosen film → Enjoys exceptional cinema without leaving StreamLine.
  • Internal State: Feels satisfied → Rediscovers StreamLine's value as a trusted film destination.
  • Pain Point Addressed: Abandoned browsing and competitor switching → More rewarding viewing experiences within StreamLine.

Three competitive advantages over the manual workaround

  1. Integrated discovery: Curated films within StreamLine → Eliminates switching between Google, Letterboxd and streaming platforms.
  2. Trusted expertise: Human-curated collections with editorial context → Reduces research effort and builds confidence.
  3. Faster decisions: Relevant, focused selections → Reduces choice overload and gets Marcus watching sooner.

Strategic outcome: Spotlight shifts StreamLine's competitive advantage from having the most content to helping film lovers discover the best content, supporting stronger engagement, differentiation and potentially improved retention.

Note: This is a proposed future-state journey, not a validated outcome. Its impact on discovery time, viewing engagement and retention requires testing.
